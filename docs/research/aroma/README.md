## The brand today

- **Two branches** (Instagram bio, Facebook):
  - **Mivida — The Lake District**, New Cairo. On the water; a fountain in the lake; white marble tables, dark rattan chairs, a wooden deck with metal railings. Guests enter Mivida from **gates 6 or 7**; breakfast is open to all guests on weekends *(social, Nov 2025)*. Google: 4.7★, "closes 1:30 AM". Coordinates 30.0082518, 31.5365496.
  - **Madinaty — The Strip Mall**, unit B11. Opened on a Friday in June 2026 ("Madinaty, we're finally here!"). Google: 4.9★. Coordinates 30.0724617, 31.6548246.
- **History:** started as a rooftop lounge at City Stars (PRD: 2006, 1,200 m² with a 900 m² terrace; client deck). City Stars is no longer a branch.
- **Bio line:** "We believe in quality ingredients. We believe in quality times. We offer both at Mivida (Lake District) branch and Madinaty (Strip Mall) branches."
- **Recurring nights** *(social)*:
  - Live guitar by Sherif Elgesr, 9–11 PM: Thursdays at Madinaty, Fridays at Mivida.
  - "Aroma Nights" with The Yellow Duck DJ: "a night of fine dining, music & good vibes".
  - Egypt match screenings on big screens, with savoury/sweet feteer and shisha-head offers.
- **Phone seen in a reel:** 012 2229 3914 (RSVP for live music) *(social; confirm)*.
- **Also on:** talabat (link in the Instagram bio).

## What it looks like (from the photos and videos)

- **Daylight by the lake**: strong sun, long shadows, golden hour over the water and the fountain; white tables; branded paper placemats with a green leaf pattern; wooden boards engraved "Aroma Lounge"; staff in leather aprons with the logo.
- **The signature drink**: layered iced teas and juices, red at the bottom and orange to gold above, held up against the sun. It appears in more posts than any dish.
- **The cappuccino**: the logo's petal flower stencilled in cocoa on the foam ("Because every cup deserves beauty").
- **Tables seen from above**, filling up with plates for sharing: feteer, beyda, shakshuka in the pan, breakfast trays, tacos, paella.
- **Night**: dark green light, plants, warm lamps, the script sign glowing; guitar on a stool.
- **Reels**: framed in flat logo green (#8FC741-ish) with white type; short lines like "Crafted with care", "Be messy", "Refined start / Perfect ending".
- **Seasonal**: Eid (coffee for dates), New Year, World Cup.
